# MOHD AZEEM

I am an emerging social science researcher interested in Indian microdata, digitalisation, society, and public policy. I use data analysis to explore employment, household consumption, inequality, livelihoods, and social and economic change across India.

I am particularly interested in datasets such as the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS), the Household Consumption Expenditure Survey (HCES), and other sources that help us understand how people live and how policies and technologies shape their lives. I enjoy comparing states and regions, with a broad focus on India. I also have a personal interest in exploring data about Rae Bareli and Uttar Pradesh.

## Research interests

- Labour, employment, and livelihoods
- Household consumption and inequality
- Rural development and agriculture
- Health, education, and digitalisation
- Society, economics, and public policy
- Indian microdata and regional comparisons

## Projects

I am building this portfolio as I learn data analysis, statistical tools, visualisation, and reproducible research. My first planned project is an exploratory analysis of India’s HCES 2023–24 household expenditure data. I will document the data sources, methods, and limitations alongside any findings.

This GitHub profile will grow with my research and learning journey.
