# Digital agriculture in India: e-NAM

This project makes two official e-NAM State/UT tables easier to explore. Both CSVs preserve the source snapshot date: **30 June 2025**.

## Data

- [Cumulative trade and mandi integration](data/enam_state_trade_since_inception_2025-06-30.csv) — 27 State/UT records, including integrated markets, trade quantity and value.
- [Registered farmers](data/enam_farmers_registered_2025-06-30.csv) — registered farmers by State/UT.

At the national level, the Government reported 1,522 integrated mandis, 17,941,613 registered farmers, 267,719 traders, 4,518 farmer producer organisations and 116,042 commission agents. Cumulative trade value was ₹439,940.97 crore. Countable commodities are reported in units separately from commodities measured in metric tonnes.

## How to interpret these figures

Trade and registration totals are cumulative platform statistics. They do not show annual activity, unique active users, farmer income, welfare or whether registration led to a sale. A blank value represents a dash in the source table; it does not mean zero. The figures are transcribed as published, including values that may warrant a source check before analysis.

This is an initial e-NAM dataset, not a complete account of agricultural digitalisation in India. It contains no farmer-level records or microdata.
