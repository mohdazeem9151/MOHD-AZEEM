# PLFS annual headline indicators (2017–18 to 2023–24)

**Start here:** [Download the CSV](data/all_india_annual_indicators_age15plus_2017-18_to_2023-24.csv) · [Source tables](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/publication_reports/Sarvekshna_117th_24022025.pdf)

This file contains 378 published all-India rate observations: LFPR, worker population ratio (WPR) and unemployment rate (UR), by year, sex, rural/urban/combined area and two reference statuses. Values are percentages for people aged 15 and above, rounded to one decimal place.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| LFPR | Labour force (working or seeking/available for work) as a share of the population |
| WPR | Employed people as a share of the population |
| UR | Unemployed people as a share of the labour force |
| usual_status_ps_ss | Usual status: principal plus subsidiary status |
| current_weekly_status | Current weekly status (CWS) |

The CSV reproduces MoSPI's time-series tables 1–3: LFPR, WPR and UR respectively (PDF pp. 92–94; printed pp. 86–89). The combined rural–urban estimate is MoSPI's published estimate, not an average calculated here. This is a summary of headline annual tables, **not the underlying PLFS microdata or every table in the reports**. Do not infer causation from rate changes.

**2025 series:** MoSPI redesigned PLFS from January 2025. Treat its results as a separate series; do not append them to this historical series without a documented comparability assessment.
