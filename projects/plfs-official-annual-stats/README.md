# PLFS annual indicators, 2017–18 to 2023–24

[Download the CSV](data/all_india_annual_indicators_age15plus_2017-18_to_2023-24.csv) · [MoSPI source tables](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/publication_reports/Sarvekshna_117th_24022025.pdf)

The CSV contains **378 published observations** for people aged 15 and above. It covers all-India LFPR, worker population ratio (WPR) and unemployment rate (UR), broken down by year, sex, rural/urban/combined area and reference status.

## Read the data

- **LFPR:** people working or seeking/available for work as a share of the population.
- **WPR:** employed people as a share of the population.
- **UR:** unemployed people as a share of the labour force.
- **Usual status (ps+ss):** principal plus subsidiary status.
- **Current weekly status (CWS):** status during the reference week.

Rates are percentages rounded to one decimal place. The combined rural–urban figure is MoSPI’s published estimate, not an average calculated in this project.

## Coverage and comparability

This is a summary of headline annual tables, not the underlying microdata or every table in the PLFS reports. MoSPI redesigned PLFS from January 2025. Keep the new series separate unless a documented assessment establishes comparability. See the [2025 annual report](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/publications_reports/publications_reports1780040415321_0624fb13-fb47-40bc-b470-7c7e9635c3ef_PLFS_2025_F_REV_29052026.pdf) and the [PLFS catalogue](https://esankhyiki.mospi.gov.in/catalogue-main/catalogue?product=PLFS).
