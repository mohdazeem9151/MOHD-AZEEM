01 / ABOUT

Curious about the systems behind social change.

My academic interests bring together digital society and social research. I study how technologies and public programmes influence access to work, credit, healthcare and other services.

My experience includes field research and data work in Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand. I am especially interested in careful, transparent research that connects evidence with the realities people face.
