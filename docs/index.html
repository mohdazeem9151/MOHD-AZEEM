01ABOUT

Curiosity for the systems behind social change.

My work brings together digital society and social research. I’m interested in how technologies and public programmes influence access to work, credit, healthcare and essential services.

Through field research and data analysis, I aim to make complex social questions clearer and more useful. My experience includes work in Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand, with a personal interest in Rae Bareli and Uttar Pradesh.
